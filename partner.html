The partner

Capital and vision

We are looking for entrepreneurs or professionals interested in contributing capital in exchange for an ownership stake and a share of profits, on terms to be agreed.

A partner who shares the goals of the project without having to manage day-to-day operations.

The JOIA team

Management and operations

The JOIA team handles day-to-day management: organising work, overseeing the product and service, and looking after customers.

Goals, communication and updates on business performance are agreed with the partner.

The investment amount, ownership stake and profit-sharing arrangements will be defined after reviewing the project and its figures. Returns are not guaranteed.

Where we start

  2. 02
